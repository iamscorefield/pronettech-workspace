Pronet-Tech Global Limited Partnership & Operations Agreement

About Pronet-Tech Global Limited

Pronet-Tech Global Limited is an organization dedicated to empowering individuals through practical education and digital skills development, as well as an online and network marketing business (2-in-1 business combining freelancing and network marketing)[cite: 6].

1. Organization Rules & Confidentiality

All business plans, strategies, and related information must be treated as confidential. Members must not disclose proprietary information to unauthorized third parties[cite: 7]. Violation of the Operations Manual may result in permanent termination of membership, deactivation of account, loss of access to team support, wallet funds, and training benefits[cite: 7, 8]. Existing members are automatically leaders to new members based on seniority joining date[cite: 8].

2. Payment and Funds Regulation Rules

A 10% monthly charge shall be paid by each member separately in addition to monthly membership fees toward team development and office maintenance[cite: 9]. Full disclosure of financial info is required to sponsors and leaders[cite: 9].

3. Account Creation & Prospecting Rules

Members must complete required training (Email marketing, Beta Reading, Video editing, Website design, or AI automation) before account creation[cite: 10]. Strict prohibition against using another person's image or creating fake/unauthorized accounts; each member is permitted only one account[cite: 10].

4. Team Leadership & Responsibility Rules

Team leaders must complete training programs and possess necessary support tools[cite: 11]. Members earning through Fiverr or approved platforms contribute 10% of earnings for office maintenance[cite: 12]. Members choosing to leave must settle applicable fees ($52/month) before retaining tools or resources acquired through PNT[cite: 12].

5. 3-Year Partnership & Exclusivity Agreement

By proceeding, you agree to fully partner with ProNet-Tech Global Limited for at least three (3) years, dedicating time and resources exclusively[cite: 13]. If terminating partnership early, you agree to surrender all tools and resources (mobiles, laptops, etc.) acquired through the business opportunity[cite: 13]. You operate as an independent contractor[cite: 13].

6. Fiverr Account Operating Rules & Asset Declaration

Profile display names must match your actual name. Accounts must be Nigerian. Do not use another person's image. Maintain professionalism and do not sell illegal services (spells, drugs, fraud, etc.). All assets/tools acquired using PNT funds remain property of Pronet-Tech Global Limited subject to recovery upon departure[cite: 14].
